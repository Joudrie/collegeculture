Social life Dorms freshman year, then apartments and small friend groups

Freshman year in the dorms is the most social it gets. After that, most people move into apartments in Mission Hill, Fenway and nearby neighborhoods, and the scene becomes small friend groups and apartment parties.

Co-op is the big shake-up. Half your friends might be working full time while you're in class, then you swap. Some people love the reset. Others say it's hard to keep a group together.

Boston fills in the gaps. Fenway Park is a walk away, the T gets you anywhere, and schools like BU and Berklee are close enough that social circles mix.

Getting in Very selective now. Apply early if it's your top pick.
  • It's hard to get into.Most applicants are turned away. Treat it as a reach unless your grades are near the top of your class.
  • Early application matters.It has early decision and early action rounds. If it's your first choice, applying early can help.
  • Tests may be optional.It has been test-optional in recent years. Check the current policy before you apply.
  • You might get NUin.Some admits spend their first semester at a partner campus, often abroad, then come to Boston in the spring. It's a real admission.
Who goes here Driven, career-minded, lots of business and engineering

Driven people who already have a rough idea of what job they want. Lots of business, engineering, computer science, health science and nursing students. There's a big international crowd and plenty of kids from New York, New Jersey and the rest of the Northeast.

It's not a "figure yourself out for four years" school. Undecided students do fine, but they'll feel pressure to pick a direction sooner than at a liberal arts college.

Known for Co-op, getting very hard to get into, and hockey
  • Co-opYou alternate between classes and 4 to 6 month stretches working a real job, often paid. Most students do two or three. It's the whole identity of the school.
  • A fast climbTwenty years ago it was a backup school. Now it turns away most applicants.
  • HockeyThe Beanpot tournament against BU, BC and Harvard is the one time a year the whole school cares about sports.
Stereotypes vs. reality "Career-obsessed" is mostly true. "Safety school" is outdated.

  • Everyone's obsessed with their career.

    Mostly true

    It's the point of the school. People talk about co-op interviews the way other schools talk about parties. The upside is you leave with a real resume.

  • There's no school spirit.

    Half true

    Football was cut in 2009, so there are no fall game days. Hockey brings people out, but day to day it's quieter than BC or UMass.

  • It's a safety school.

    Outdated

    True for your parents' generation. Today it's one of the harder schools in the state to get into.

  • It doesn't feel like a real campus.

    Half true

    The center of campus has green space and is pretty nice. Walk a block away and you're in the city with no clear edge.

Myths and things to know Plan for 5 years, Boston is expensive, no car needed

Myths you might hear

  • It's a 4-year school.Most people take 5 years because of co-op. There's a 4-year track with fewer co-ops. Plan for the cost of the extra year.
  • Co-op hands you a job.The school helps, but you apply and interview like anyone else. Your first co-op might not be your dream company.

Things to know before you go

  • Your friend group will shift.Co-op schedules split people up every few months. Clubs and roommates become the steady part.
  • Boston is expensive.Most upperclassmen live off campus, and rent nearby is high. The sticker price isn't the whole cost.
  • You don't need a car.The Green Line E branch and the Orange Line both stop by campus.
  • Look at net price, not sticker price.What families pay after aid varies a lot. Use the net price calculator on the school's website.
Is it for you? Great if you want a job lined up. Rough if you want tailgates.

You'll probably love it if

  • You want work experience before you graduate
  • You want to live in a real city
  • You're fine making your own social scene

You might struggle if

  • You want game days and tailgates
  • You want the same friend group all four years
  • You want time to explore before picking a path
