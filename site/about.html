College Culture About

A quick, honest look at what New England colleges are like. Not rankings, not advice.

What this is

Brochures and tours show every school at its best. This site tries to tell you what a place actually feels like: the vibe, the social life, the stereotypes and whether they hold up, and who tends to love it or struggle there. It's a starting point for your list, not the final word.

Where the info comes from

  • Numbers. Undergrad size, admit rate, prices, graduation rates and pay after college come from the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard, pulled October 2026.
  • Vibe, stereotypes and myths. Summaries of what students and alumni commonly say in public, written with help from AI. They are generalizations, so any one student's experience can be different.
  • Colors and logos. From each school's brand guide where we could find it, otherwise from public sources like Wikipedia.

What it is not

  • Not a ranking. A school that fits one person is wrong for another.
  • Not advice. Talk to your counselor, visit if you can, and check each school's own website.
  • Not connected to any college. No school paid to be here or saw its page first.

The quiz

The quiz sorts schools by how well they match your answers. It's a rough sort, so treat the results as names to look into. Your answers stay on your device. If you tap Share, the link holds your answers and a rough home area, never your ZIP code.

Things change

Prices go up, admit rates move and campus culture shifts. If something here looks wrong, trust the school's own numbers.
